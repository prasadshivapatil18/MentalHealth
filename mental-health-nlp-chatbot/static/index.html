VALIDATED CLINICAL TOOL

Patient Health Questionnaire-4 (PHQ-4)

An ultra-brief 4-item screener measuring the frequency of anxiety and depressive symptoms over the last 2 weeks. Used widely in epidemiological studies and the CDC Household Pulse Survey.

Instructions: Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems?
Q1 ANXIETY SUBSCALE

Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge

Q2 ANXIETY SUBSCALE

Not being able to stop or control worrying

Q3 DEPRESSION SUBSCALE

Little interest or pleasure in doing things

Q4 DEPRESSION SUBSCALE

Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless
